## How to agree the final matrix

1. Each member checks the description, preferred name and account association. In particular, confirm the M5 display-name variation against roles.md rather than assuming that different strings prove different people.
2. Add missing evidence for analysis, pair work, meetings, reviews and testing; identify shared work and avoid attributing the same original service/model to the integrator who copied it into the review branch.
3. Discuss effort, responsibility, quality and participation together. Record a proposed percentage for each member and confirm the total is 100. No numerical allocation is inferred here from incomplete evidence.
4. Transfer the agreed information to the existing `docs/contribution-matrix-template.docx` in the repository. Members sign individually; include the agreement date and supporting references.

Roles are evidence of planned responsibility, not proof every activity was completed. Runtime tests in this session were AI-assisted under Omar's direction and are recorded as such; they must not be attributed as personally performed work by another member. No retrospective attendance record or signature is created by this proposal.
